# AJDM Privacy Policy

**Draft — 18 October 2026.** This is a working draft for review. It is not legal advice. Items in [square brackets] must be completed.

## The short version

AJDM runs on your computer. It has no account, no advertising, no analytics and no telemetry. It does not send your downloads, your history or your settings to the author or to anyone else. Network connections are made only to the websites you download from, plus the specific services listed below.

## 1. What AJDM keeps on your computer

To work, AJDM stores on your own computer, in your user profile, and only readable by your user account where the system allows:

- **Your download list:** addresses of the files you add, where they are saved, their progress and state, so downloads can resume after a restart.
- **Your settings:** folders, connection counts, queues, schedules, speed limits and window choices.
- **Data usage:** how many bytes were downloaded each day, if you use the daily data limit.
- **Temporary video information:** the qualities of a video page you opened, kept in memory for up to 10 minutes so starting the download is instant.

You can delete downloads from the list at any time. Uninstalling AJDM and deleting its profile folder removes this information.

## 2. Network connections AJDM makes

- **The sites you download from.** To download a file, AJDM connects to the server that hosts it, like a browser does. That server sees your IP address and the request, as it would for any download.
- **Video pages.** To list a video's qualities, AJDM runs yt-dlp on your computer, which connects to the video site.
- **TikTok links (public videos only).** For public TikTok links, AJDM may also ask the public TikWM and Douyin services for the video's information. They receive the video's page address. Your cookies or sign-in details are never sent to them.
- **Nothing else.** AJDM does not contact the author's servers. It does not check for updates in the background.

## 3. Sign-in information (optional)

Only if you choose it in Options → Video sites, AJDM uses a signed-in browser session or a cookies.txt file you select, so that private or members-only videos can be read. These cookies:

- are read on your computer only when analysing a video or renewing its download link;
- are sent only to the site they belong to, as your browser would;
- are never stored by AJDM. AJDM remembers only the browser's name or the file's path.

Turn it off at any time with "Stop using sign-in".

## 4. The browser extension

The AJDM extension works only with the AJDM app installed on the same computer:

- It sees the downloads you start in the browser, so it can hand them to AJDM (with their address, the page they came from, the file name and size). If AJDM is not running, the download continues in the browser.
- It notices video and audio streams that pages play, to list them under "Download this video". This list stays in the browser's memory for that tab and is cleared when you leave the page.
- It sends information only to the AJDM app on your computer, through the browser's native messaging. It does not send anything over the internet itself, and it does not collect your browsing history.
- Its settings (capture on or off, minimum size, excluded sites) are stored in the browser's extension storage.

## 5. Children

AJDM is not directed at children under 13 and collects no personal information from anyone.

## 6. Security

AJDM keeps its files private to your user account where possible, talks to its own background service only through a local, private channel, and refuses to start if its data folder could be read by other users.

## 7. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published with AJDM and on the project page, with a new date.
